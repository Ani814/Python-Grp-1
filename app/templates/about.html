About JobBoard

JobBoard is a job-listing platform built with Flask for the Tbilisi School of Communication Python final project. Anyone can browse open positions without an account; registered users can post, edit, and manage their own job listings.

Built with Flask, Jinja2, Bootstrap, Flask-Login, Flask-WTF, and SQLAlchemy. Salary figures are converted live using the Frankfurter currency API.
